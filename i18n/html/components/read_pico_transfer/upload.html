Upload fonts

Checking font upload requirements…


Choose a complete Chinese TrueType (TTF) font that you have the right to use. After it uploads, stop Transfer and select it on the device’s Font page. Replacing a font asks for confirmation; the device uses its built-in font during Transfer.

Upload comics

Checking comic upload requirements…



Before uploading, this phone converts every page to a grayscale JPEG sized to the device screen and cuts very long strips into screens; files shrink to about 1/5 and pages turn faster on the device. Keep this page open and the screen on while converting.

Stopping on the device or leaving the Transfer page interrupts the current upload; a file that is unfinished or fails verification never replaces the original.
