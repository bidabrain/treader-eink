Read Pico · open-source reading firmware · Android apps

A reading system
redesigned for e-ink

Local TXT / EPUB, CBZ comics and Tomato (Fanqie novels), plus RedNote, Weibo and Hupu, all laid out anew for a 4.7-inch 16-level grayscale screen and the ESP32-S3. Flash it right in your browser; the firmware file never leaves your computer.

It is more than firmware for the Pico: Tomato, RedNote and Weibo each have an Android app with grayscale pages, page-by-page turning and the same layout as the device, so your phone reads like an e-ink screen too. Tomato reading progress and bookshelf also sync with the Pico over WebDAV. There are offline two-step codes as well: the Authenticator on the device, plus an Android app that can send accounts to the Pico.

FEATURES

One reader with everything it should have

Every list turns by the page, no scrolling. Online content is cached to the SD card first, so whatever you've downloaded still works offline. The bottom bar holds the features you use most and the rest live under "All"; if text feels small, set the display size to Large or Extra large.

Library & reading

TXT and EPUB shelf with covers and pinyin-initial search; adjustable line spacing, paragraph spacing and margins, per-book progress, and timed auto page turning.

Comics

CBZ organized by series, one page per screen, and the next chapter starts when you finish one; web uploads are converted to grayscale first so pages turn faster.

Tomato (Fanqie novels)

Search, download and check for updates, and browse the Store by ranking and category; downloaded books read in the Library like any other book. An Android app does the same on your phone.

RedNote

Followed creators and the home feed as two-column cover cards; images one per screen, then the text and comments. Large images can be saved as the lock screen wallpaper.

Via a self-hosted rednote server

Weibo

People you follow and trending posts, with card layout, gray boxes for reposts and image grids; long posts get their full text and comments load on demand. No Weibo sign-in needed.

Via a self-hosted weibo server

Hupu

Recommended, followed boards, scores and player ratings, threads and top-voted replies; no sign-in and no server needed.

Transfer

Upload books, fonts and comics from a web page over Wi-Fi, or connect a USB cable and copy straight to the SD card as a USB drive.

Sync & updates

WebDAV syncs reading progress and the Tomato bookshelf. Tap "Check for updates" on the device to upgrade online (downloaded to the SD card first, resumes after interruptions, written only after it verifies), or put the firmware on the SD card to upgrade; if it fails to boot, it rolls back to the previous version automatically.

Discover comics · follow WEBTOON

Open "Discover" at the top right of My comics to browse WEBTOON rankings, genres, the weekday schedule and completed series, or search by title (Simplified Chinese input also finds the Traditional Chinese edition). After subscribing, tap "Check for updates" and new episodes download as CBZ into My comics, to read alongside your local comics. An Android app does the same on your phone.

Setup guide Android app
  • The server turns each episode's long vertical strip into grayscale and cuts it into screens in the blank gaps between panels, never splitting a panel or speech bubble in half; pages turn as fast as local comics
  • Subscriptions live only on the device's SD card (a marker file in the title's folder); the server keeps none, so changing servers or reinstalling loses nothing
  • Filter My comics by All / Local / WEBTOON; subscribed covers are tagged "WT", and newly downloaded unread episodes show "N new"
  • When the server has Mihon extension sources enabled, the Discover page can switch source, browse by Popular / Latest and search within the current source; subscriptions and downloads work the same
  • Only fetches episodes that are free and public on the website, with no sign-in and nothing paid; deploy it yourself and use it only for yourself

Authenticator · two-step codes

Shows TOTP two-step verification codes offline, the same ones Google Authenticator computes, without connecting to any server. It is under "All" since 1.0.0; an Android app does the same on your phone and can send accounts straight to Read Pico.

User guide Android app
  • Accounts are encrypted on the SD card with the Authenticator passphrase and can only be decrypted with that passphrase on this device; the passphrase is separate from the lock screen passcode
  • Import: the device starts a hotspot, and on your phone's web page you scan a Google Authenticator export QR code, paste an otpauth link or type in a secret; nothing is written until you tap "Import" on the device
  • It locks on every lock screen and restart, and can also lock 5 minutes after you leave the Authenticator; repeated wrong passphrases make you wait, doubling each time
  • Always keep the authenticator on your phone as a backup: secrets cannot be exported and there is no cloud sync, so a forgotten passphrase means wiping and starting over

ECOSYSTEM

Your own servers, your own data

The device never talks to Tomato, RedNote or Weibo directly; it only connects to servers you deploy yourself with Docker, each with a web panel and a Cloudflare tunnel. The RedNote and Weibo servers also turn images into grayscale that the e-ink screen can show as is. Each of the three servers has an Android app whose features and layout follow the firmware: black, white and gray, turning by the page. It works on your phone without a Pico, and with a Pico you can use both.

tomato · Tomato download server

  • The official Tomato-Novel-Downloader plus a panel, deployed with one compose file
  • The device password is managed in the panel, and only the device API is exposed to the internet
  • Android app: Library, search and download, Store and page-by-page reading; progress and bookshelf sync with the Pico over WebDAV
Setup guide Android app

rednote · RedNote server

  • Sign in to the RedNote website by scanning a QR code; it regularly fetches posts from creators you follow
  • The home feed is only read when you tap "Refresh" on the device
  • The follow list can be backed up and restored with WebDAV; the tunnel exposes only the device API
Setup guide Android app

weibo · Weibo server

  • No sign-in; fetches people you follow and trending posts on a schedule as a visitor
  • Skips posts with video, fills in the full text of long posts, and loads comments on demand
  • The follow list shares one WebDAV backup file with weiboX
Setup guide Android app

webtoon · Discover comics server

  • Reads free public WEBTOON episodes, with no sign-in and no stored subscriptions
  • Converts an episode only when the device asks for it: grayscale, cut into screens in the blank gaps between panels, packed as CBZ
  • Optional extension sources: Suwayomi runs Mihon extensions, with repositories and extensions managed in the panel; this project ships no repository, so add only sources you have the right to access
  • Deploy: download docker-compose.yml, then docker compose up -d; for extension sources, put COMPOSE_PROFILES=mihon in a .env in the same folder and run up -d again, or use mihon,flaresolverr when sites need to pass a Cloudflare check. Upgrade: download the new compose file, run docker compose pull, then up -d; all data is kept
  • Android app: Discover page, subscriptions, update checks and one-page-per-screen reading
Setup guide Android app
INSTALL

Flash from your browser

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer and connect Read Pico with a USB-C data cable; three steps and you're done. Read Pico has no BOOT button, so the page puts it into download mode automatically.

Download the latest firmware

GitHub Actions builds and publishes the firmware automatically after every change, with the same configuration as the real device, so it can be flashed straight onto Read Pico. These are the English builds; after downloading, drag the file into step 1 below.

Full image (first flash) Upgrade package SD card files All versions ›

The first flash also needs an SD card: download the "SD card files" (treader-<version>-sdcard.zip, with the fonts and the pinyin dictionary), unzip it and copy everything inside to the root of a FAT32 SD card (not the unzipped folder itself). Without it the UI is missing characters and you can't type Chinese. Later firmware upgrades don't need it copied again.

Language: online updates on the English firmware install only English packages, and the Chinese firmware only Chinese ones. To switch language, flash the other build; an upgrade keeps your settings and reading progress.

Tomato needs a self-hosted server

The downloadable firmware has no Tomato download server built in. Reading, comics, Hupu, Store rankings and Transfer all work out of the box; to search and download Tomato novels, use this project's one-step Tomato server (the official Tomato-Novel-Downloader plus a panel; docker compose up -d is all it takes), then enter the address and password shown in the panel under "Settings › Content sources › Tomato" on the device. RedNote, Weibo and WEBTOON Discover comics each need a self-hosted server too (see "Servers & apps" above).

Store rankings won't load?

By default the Store fetches rankings from GitHub Pages (bidabrain.github.io), which is often unreachable from mainland China. On the device, under "Settings › Content sources › Tomato › Store rankings" (in the Tomato Android app, "Settings › Store rankings"), change the address to this jsDelivr mirror:

https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/bidabrain/FanqieRankTracker@main

The mirror can lag by up to half a day. You can also fork FanqieRankTracker to host your own rankings (turn on GitHub Pages, then enter your own site address); see Store rankings.

Flashing notes

          
    
            
  • Click "Connect device and start" and pick the device's serial port in the popup (usually called USB JTAG/serial debug unit).
    • 
            
  • Writing takes about 1 to 2 minutes; don't unplug the cable until it finishes. The device restarts by itself when done.
    • 
            
  • Read Pico has no BOOT button, so you don't need to press anything. The device must be powered on (USB doesn't work after choosing "Power off" on the lock screen).
    • 
            
  • Can't connect: try a cable that carries data (USB-A to C is officially recommended); close programs using the serial port (serial monitors, other flasher pages); unplug and replug USB; put the device to sleep and wake it once, or power it off and on again.
    • 
            
  • Use the treader-<version>-full-en.bin downloaded above, or build/treader-full.bin (build/Read_Pico.bin) if you build it yourself. When building yourself you can build in a Tomato server, see the project README; don't publish firmware built that way.
    • 
            
  • After the first flash, unzip the "SD card files" downloaded above and copy everything inside to the root of the SD card: fonts/ (fonts) and ime/ (pinyin dictionary) are required; without them characters are missing and you can't type Chinese.
